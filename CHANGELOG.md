## 1.1.0

## 1.0.0

* Initial Flutter port of the Swift Notelet package: list, image and video notes (images and videos from the network or app assets), `NoteletSheet` and `showNoteletSheet()`, `NoteletConfiguration` (button and screen reader labels, accent color, sheet height), JSON decoding and `NoteletStorage`.
